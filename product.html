Hardware · Wrist pod

One board, exploded.

The wrist pod is a three-part assembly: a top shell, the main board, and a base shell with an optical window aligned to the PPG sensor. Below is the actual board — not a mockup.

The board that runs the wrist pod: two QFP controllers, a BGA sensor-front-end IC, a diode, the optical PPG module, and a regulator — sandwiched between a top shell and a base shell with a window aligned straight to the sensor.

Reflectance mode

Light enters the tissue and returns through the same window — three wavelengths, 940 nm infrared penetrating deepest.

One board, one job

The wrist pod's controller handles PPG capture and timing sync with the earlobe clip over the shared hardware clock.
