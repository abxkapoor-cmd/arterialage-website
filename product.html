How it works

A wearable that watches your arteries — not just your pulse.

A chest hub and two optical sensors — wrist and earlobe — sharing one hardware clock. The gap between the pulse arriving at each site is timed to sub-millisecond precision, and an onboard model turns that into an HFpEF risk score. No cuff, no needle, no clinic visit.

Worn, not implanted: the same three parts — earlobe clip, chest hub, wrist pod — shown in place on the body.

14 days, worn at home

Posted out, worn, returned — then cleaned and redeployed to the next patient.

Additive, not disruptive

Sits before ECG and echo. Replaces nothing already in the pathway.

Earlier therapy, fewer admissions

Earlier detection means earlier SGLT2 treatment — worth ~26% fewer hospitalisations.

Hardware · Wrist pod

The wrist pod, exploded.

Strap, base shell, board, top shell — the wrist pod is four parts. The base shell's window sits directly over the board's optical module, against the skin.

Four parts, one pod: a silicone strap, a base shell whose window sits directly over the board's optical module, the board itself, and a top shell.

Reflectance mode

Light enters the tissue and returns through the same window — three wavelengths, 940 nm infrared penetrating deepest.

One board, one job

The wrist pod's controller handles PPG capture and timing sync with the earlobe clip over the shared hardware clock.

Shared design

The earlobe clip's outer board reuses this same wrist board — one design, built once, used twice.

Hardware · Earlobe clip

The earlobe clip, exploded.

A clip, not a case: the earlobe sits between two thin shell surfaces, with the outer shell and board on one side and the smaller inner board and shell on the other. The outer side reuses the wrist pod's board; the inner side carries a smaller board, sized for the tighter space.

Seven parts, one clip: outer shell and board on one side, inner board and shell on the other, with two thin clip surfaces pinching the earlobe between them — transmission mode, light passing straight through.

Transmission mode

Light passes straight through the earlobe, giving a cleaner pulse waveform at the site the pulse reaches first.

Two boards, one clip

The larger board (shared with the wrist pod) sits outer-side; a smaller board sits inner-side, in the tighter space.

Same shared clock

Both boards sync to the same hardware clock as the wrist pod, timing the pulse between the two sites.
