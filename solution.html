The solution

A wearable that watches your arteries — not just your pulse.

A passive 14-day home wearable that measures arterial stiffness by timing the pulse between two body sites, then applies a machine-learning model to classify HFpEF risk from six physiological features. No cuff, no needle, no clinic visit.

Early detection

Built for everyone the BP monitor was never designed for

Identifies HFpEF risk early by tracking arterial stiffness through pulse wave velocity — the signal a cuff cannot see, in the patients a cuff was never calibrated for.

Passive, at home

Fourteen days, no clinic visit

Posted out, worn, returned. The device is then cleaned, re-padded, tested and redeployed to the next patient — so one unit screens patient after patient.

Additive, not disruptive

Runs alongside the standard workup

An upstream screening layer that sits before ECG and echo. It does not replace any existing test — which is what makes it adoptable inside a pathway nobody wants to rebuild.

The product

Three parts: one hub, two vascular vantage points.

A chest hub carrying the processor, battery and radio, plus two optical PPG sensors — one at the wrist, one at the earlobe. Both share a hardware exposure clock, so the difference in pulse arrival between the two sites can be timed to sub-millisecond precision. That difference is pulse transit time, and from it, pulse wave velocity.

The pulse leaves the heart and travels two arterial paths of unequal length. It reaches the earlobe first and the wrist second; Δt is that difference. Timing comes from the shared clock across two optical sensors — there is no ECG channel in this device, and no ECG signal is consumed, duplicated or replaced.

Chest hub

Where the model runs

Host processor, Bluetooth and battery. The six-feature HFpEF risk classifier runs on board, so the patient wears a screening device rather than a data logger.

Wrist pod

Reflectance mode

Light goes into the tissue and comes back out the same side. Three wavelengths are used — 940 nm infrared penetrates deepest and is least affected by melanin.

Earlobe clip

Transmission mode

Light passes straight through the lobe, giving a cleaner pulse waveform at the site the pulse reaches first. No cuff, no needle, no electrodes.
