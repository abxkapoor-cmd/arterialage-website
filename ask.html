The ask · Pre-seed

Raising to take Vascora Health from MVP to first paid pilots.

The NHS is not short of heart failure treatment. It is short of timely heart failure detection. That is the gap we sell into — and this round funds the two-site timing validation, the quality system, and the first clinical pilot that turns the evidence base into an NHS purchase order.

Engineering

Two-site timing validation

Demonstrating sub-millisecond cross-site timing on our own hardware — the first milestone everything else depends on.

Quality

ISO 13485 design freeze

A locked, controlled design that a UKCA technical file can be built on rather than rebuilt from.

Clinical

Ethics-approved pilot

An ambulatory cohort against reference PWV standards, including Monk-scale cross-skin-tone validation.

Commercial

First paid pilot

Private-clinic revenue, then an ICB or Community Diagnostic Centre running episodes on local budgets.
